Welcome to Agra

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is famous around the world for the beautiful Taj Mahal and its rich Mughal history.

History of Agra

Agra has played an important role in Indian history. It became an important center during the Mughal Empire. Many beautiful monuments were built during this period.

Famous Places

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world and is known for its white marble architecture.

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a historic red sandstone fort that contains beautiful palaces and halls.

Fatehpur Sikri

Fatehpur Sikri is a historic city built by Emperor Akbar near Agra.

Culture of Agra

Agra has a rich culture influenced by Mughal traditions. The city is also famous for its food, handicrafts, marble work and traditional markets.

Why Visit Agra?

Agra is a perfect destination for people interested in history, architecture, food and Indian culture. Millions of tourists visit the city every year.
